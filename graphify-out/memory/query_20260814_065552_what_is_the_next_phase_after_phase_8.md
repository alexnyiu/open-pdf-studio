The next phase is Phase 9: Accessibility and density. It is a 1 to 2 week initial audit followed by continuous work to close remaining measurable gaps: WCAG AA text contrast, keyboard access for major commands, visible focus, accessible icon names, non-color-only status cues, usability at 800x600, comfortable Retina and non-Retina rendering, and future comfortable or compact density choices. Phase 10 follows with rollout and cleanup.
